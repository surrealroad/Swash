Math editing: inside `$…$`, `$$` blocks and math code blocks, typing `{` inserts a matching `}` (wrapping any selection), and typing `}` steps over an existing one. After a backslash, the system completion shortcut (Esc or ⌥Esc) lists KaTeX commands such as `\frac`, `\alpha` and `\rightarrow`.
