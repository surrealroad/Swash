Render math and Mermaid diagrams offline with bundled KaTeX and Mermaid. In the Preview and Quick Look, `$$` blocks, inline `$…$` math and ```` ```mermaid ```` code blocks are typeset or drawn as images. Edit Text shows the rendering under the editable source, keeps the last good result while you type, and shows KaTeX or Mermaid errors inline.
