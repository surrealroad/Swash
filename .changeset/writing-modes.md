New writing modes in the View menu on the Mac. Focus Mode (⇧⌘D) dims everything except the paragraph or sentence you're writing (choose which under Focus On). Typewriter Scrolling (⌃⌘T) keeps the line you're typing in the middle of the window. Distraction-Free Writing (⌃⇧⌘F) goes full screen, hides the toolbar, outline and status bar, and centres the text in a readable column; press Esc to leave it.
