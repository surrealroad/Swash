The iPhone and iPad editor gains the rest of the Mac's editing interactions. You can tap a code block's language badge to choose its language. Typing "/" at the start of an empty line offers block types: a menu at the caret on iPad, or a bar above the keyboard on iPhone. Table cells can be edited in place, moving between cells with Tab or the keyboard toolbar. Copying from the editor puts rich text and the Markdown source on the clipboard, and pasting rich text from other apps converts it to Markdown.
