Swash now builds for iPhone and iPad as well as the Mac, as a first step towards a universal app (see `docs/UNIVERSAL_PLAN.md`). On iOS and iPadOS you can open and create Markdown documents from the Files browser and switch between Source, Formatted (read-only for now) and, on iPad, Split views with synced scrolling. The Source editor continues lists and handles Backspace and Tab like the Mac editor, has a formatting bar above the keyboard and formatting actions in the edit menu, and supports the Format menu on iPad. The Mac app is unchanged.
