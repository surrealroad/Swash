---
"swash": minor
---

Swash on iPhone and iPad now works with the rest of the system. `swash://new` and `swash://open` links, the Quick Note widget and the Create Markdown Document shortcut open their note directly, even with another document open. Other apps can share text or links to Swash with "Save to Swash", and the note opens the next time you switch to Swash. The share button no longer crashes on iPhone. On the Mac, Finder thumbnails for Markdown files no longer crash.
